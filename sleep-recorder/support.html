Sleep Recorder – Support

Need help? Email mobileappssupport0@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Common questions

The app didn't record anything. Check that microphone access is on (iOS Settings > Sleep Recorder), keep the app recording until morning, and try a higher sensitivity.

Where are my recordings? On your phone only. Open the Nights tab. Recordings from older versions appear marked "imported".

How do I delete a recording? Individual clip and whole-night deletion is coming in iOS version 2.2.4. Once that update is available and installed, open Nights and tap the trash icon beside a night, or open its report and use Delete night. To remove one audio clip, tap its trash icon in the report. Confirm the deletion when prompted. Stop an active recording before deleting its night. Deletion is permanent and also removes the selected transcript; deleting a night also removes its saved movement data.

If your installed version does not have these controls, email support for help. Your recordings are stored on your phone, so we cannot delete them remotely. Deleting the app removes all of its locally stored recordings; offloading the app keeps its data.

How do I restore Premium? In the app open Settings > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Sleep Recorder.
