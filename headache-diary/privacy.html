Headache Diary Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026. Contact: mobileappssupport0@gmail.com.

Your health diary

Episodes, intensity, medication, notes and check-ins remain in the app’s private storage. We do not operate a server receiving your diary. We do not send your diary content or health activity to advertising, analytics, subscription or crash reporting services. Starting with version 1.0 (7), the diary and its recovery directory are excluded from automatic device backups and remain protected in app-local storage. This setting is also applied to existing local diaries without deleting their entries. Older device backups or copies you previously exported may still contain health information; manage those separately. There is no automatic diary cloud sync.

Exports and deletion

PDF, CSV and backup files contain the information you enter. They leave the app when you export them. Backup files are not password protected. You can delete entries in the app; recovery copies may retain earlier records until the app is removed. Uninstalling removes local app data. Manage separately exported files and device/cloud backups where they are stored.

Advertising

The free version may show Google AdMob ads in Settings. Premium removes ads. We request non-personalized contextual ads and disable publisher first-party ID. No diary content or health keywords are included in ad requests. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device/advertising identifiers where permitted, advertising interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and protect ads. Google’s User Messaging Platform handles applicable consent and privacy choices. Apple’s tracking permission is requested only after the consent flow permits advertising. Denying tracking does not restrict the diary.

Optional usage analytics

Firebase Analytics is off until you enable “Share anonymous usage” in Settings. It measures general app readiness, Premium screen views, purchase attempts/completions and restore completions. Its SDK may also collect technical device/app information, an installation identifier, session and engagement information. We disable automatic screen reporting, IDFV collection, ad personalization and advertising consent. We do not set your name, email, account ID or diary details in analytics. You can turn usage sharing off in Settings, which stops collection and resets the local analytics identifier.

Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash diagnostics by default, including stack traces, app/OS versions, technical device information and installation/session identifiers. You can disable this in Settings. We do not add diary content, medications, notes or health events to crash reports. Previously delivered reports may remain subject to provider retention policies.

Subscriptions

Apple processes payments; we do not receive payment card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app identifier, purchase history and subscription entitlement information to validate subscriptions, support restores and provide subscription analytics. No diary content is sent to RevenueCat. Purchase records may be retained for legal and accounting obligations.

Service providers and rights

Third parties receiving data are required to provide the same or equal protection as described in this policy and required by Apple’s App Review Guidelines. We do not authorize these providers to use your diary content for advertising.

Providers may process data outside your country under their terms and applicable transfer safeguards. See Google Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy. Contact support for access, correction or deletion requests concerning service data. We do not maintain a health diary account or server copy. Avoid including health details in support messages unless you intend to share them. This app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Purpose

Headache Diary is for personal record keeping. It does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment.
