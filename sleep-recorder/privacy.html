Sleep Recorder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sleep Recorder app ("the app"), published by Batuhan Senavci, collects and how it is used.

Your recordings stay on your phone

  • The app uses the microphone only after you tap Start Recording. It saves short audio clips when it hears a sound.
  • Clips, sound labels (snoring, talking, other) and sleep-talk transcripts are created and stored on your device only. Sound analysis and speech-to-text run on the device using the phone's on-device frameworks (Apple's on iPhone, Android's on-device speech recognition on Android); your audio is never uploaded to us or to any server we control.
  • While a recording is running, the app reads your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to count how often you move. Only per-minute movement counts are saved, on your device only; they are never uploaded or shared, and the app does not ask for Motion & Fitness access.
  • Clips leave your phone only if you choose to share one yourself.
  • Deleting the app deletes your recordings.

Information collected by third-party services

  • Advertising (Google AdMob) – shown to users without a Premium subscription. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; on iOS you can allow or deny tracking; on Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics and Amplitude) – usage events such as starting a recording, opening a report or seeing an ad, the recording's length and interruptions, and your phone's battery level rounded to 10%, plus basic device information (model, OS and app version, country). Used to improve the app. Amplitude is a US company; data may be processed in the United States. No audio, transcripts, names, email addresses, precise location or advertising identifier are sent, and Amplitude does not store your IP address. If you answer the optional in-app question about why you use the app, your answer is stored only in Amplitude, not Google. Events are linked to the anonymous app user ID used for your subscription. Amplitude Privacy Notice
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) – if the app crashes, a report with device and app details is sent so the problem can be fixed.
  • Subscriptions (Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat) – purchases are processed by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app user ID to confirm your subscription. RevenueCat also sends subscription events (trial, renewal, cancellation) to Amplitude under the same anonymous ID. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Advertising measurement with Meta (iPhone)

Introduced in iPhone version 2.2.2, this optional measurement helps us understand whether our advertisements lead to app installs, trials and paid subscriptions. It uses Meta App Events and RevenueCat. A Premium subscription removes ads shown inside the app; advertising measurement follows your separate iPhone tracking permission.

We enable this measurement only when you allow tracking in iOS and the app's regional privacy check confirms that GDPR does not apply and no additional privacy choices are required. If the region cannot be confirmed, measurement remains disabled. Consent given for Google advertising does not authorize Meta measurement. Regions requiring a separate Meta consent solution remain disabled.

Meta receives app installation and activation events, an app-generated Facebook anonymous identifier and, where permitted, your device advertising identifier. RevenueCat sends Meta a hashed anonymous subscription user ID, permitted device identifiers including the vendor identifier, IP address, app/device context such as model and operating system/app version, and subscription lifecycle events with their monetary value. These events include trial starts, first paid subscriptions and renewals, and may be sent while the app is closed. Meta may combine this information with information it already holds for advertising measurement.

We do not send Meta your recordings, transcripts, sound labels, sleep reports, name, email address or phone number. You can change tracking permission in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Denying tracking does not prevent recording or buying Premium. When the app next checks permission, it stops new Meta activation events and clears the Meta matching identifiers in its RevenueCat customer attributes. This does not erase events already received by these services or cancel your subscription.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not access your recordings or transcripts.
  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email in the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off microphone or speech recognition access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, turn off microphone access in Settings > Apps > Sleep Recorder > Permissions.
  • A Premium subscription removes ads.

Not a medical device

Sleep Recorder describes sounds your phone heard. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
