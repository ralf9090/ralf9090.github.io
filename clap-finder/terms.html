No guarantee of detection

Clap Finder helps you find a phone that is nearby. Detection depends on the room, the distance and background noise; it can miss a whistle or clap and can occasionally ring by mistake. iOS stops the app from listening if the app is closed (swiped away), if the phone restarts, or while another app uses the microphone. The Motion alarm notices when the phone is moved; it is not a theft-protection or security product. Do not rely on the app where a missed or false alarm could cause harm.
