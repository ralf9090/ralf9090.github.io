Terms of Use

Promptake uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

Memberships

Plus offers an auto-renewing weekly or yearly subscription. The total price and billing period are shown in the app before purchase. Both options unlock unlimited recordings and remove advertising. Your first successfully saved recording is free; this is not a timed subscription trial.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Use Restore purchases in the app to check membership access for your Apple Account. Previously saved videos remain available to review, trim and share without an active membership.

Free recordings and advertising

The free version includes advertising. When an ad is available, you may choose to complete a rewarded ad for one additional successfully saved recording. Closing early earns no credit. A credit stays on this device until used; deleting the app may remove it. Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Ad availability is not guaranteed.
