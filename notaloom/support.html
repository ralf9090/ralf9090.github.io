Which recordings can I use?

Use your voice or a short, single-voice reference you have permission to use. You can select a WAV/M4A file up to 30 MB and a section up to 30 seconds. A reference recorded with the microphone can be up to 15 seconds. Vocals are not isolated from multi-instrument songs.

How do I practice?

Choose a phrase, listen to the reference, then record only your voice. Pitch and, when applicable, relative timing differences appear after recording. Retry the suggested section and compare takes with matching scope.

The microphone is unavailable

Check microphone access for the app in your iPhone’s Settings. If another audio app is using the microphone, close it and try again.

A recording was interrupted

Return to the app. Preserved drafts appear under Recovered recordings above your library. You can listen, share or confirm deletion. Interrupted recordings are not automatically scored.

I deleted the free exercises

Open Settings → Restore free exercises. Your own phrases and takes are not reset.

How do I restore or cancel membership?

When purchases are available, open Settings → Membership and purchases → Restore purchases. To cancel, use Manage subscriptions in the app or iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Your existing recordings remain accessible after membership ends.
