Common questions

It didn't ring. Tap Start before you put the phone down and keep the app in the background (don't swipe it away). Whistle one clear note for about a second – whistling works best. For a double clap, clap twice sharply about half a second apart. Check that microphone access is on (iOS Settings > Clap Finder) and that your volume is up.

Listening stopped. iOS stops listening when the app is closed, after a restart, or while another app (a call, a video recording) uses the microphone. Clap Finder resumes on its own after an interruption and sends a notification if it was closed.

What is the orange dot? iOS shows it whenever an app uses the microphone. It is normal while Clap Finder listens.

It rang by itself. Some sounds (music, TV, a whistling kettle) can resemble a whistle or a clap. After an alarm the app asks "Was it you?" – your answer is saved in the detection log on your phone.

How do I restore Premium? In the app open Settings > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Clap Finder.
