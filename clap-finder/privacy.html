Clap Finder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information the Clap Finder: Find Phone app ("the app"), published by Batuhan Senavci, collects and how it is used.

Sound is analyzed on your phone

  • The app uses the microphone only after you tap Start, and only to detect a whistle or a double clap. Sound is analyzed on your device with on-device models; your audio is never uploaded to us or to any server we control.
  • To help improve detection, the app keeps a detection log (event times, detector scores, battery level) and the last few seconds of sound around each detection (up to 60 short snippets) on your device only. They leave your phone only if you choose to share them yourself. You can delete them at any time in Settings > Delete detection data; deleting the app deletes them too.
  • Answers to "Was it you?" and the "I clapped, it didn't ring" button are written to the same on-device log.
  • The optional Motion alarm reads the phone's motion sensor while it is on, to notice when the phone is moved. Motion data is not stored or shared, and the app does not ask for Motion & Fitness access.

Information collected by third-party services

  • Advertising (Google AdMob) – shown to users without a Premium subscription. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; on iOS you can allow or deny tracking. Google Privacy Policy
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics) – anonymous usage events (for example, that listening was started or the paywall was shown) and basic device information, used to improve the app. No audio is sent.
  • Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat) – purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app user ID to confirm your subscription. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not receive your audio, your detection log or your location.
  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email in the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off microphone or notification access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Delete the on-device detection data in the app's Settings.
  • A Premium subscription removes ads.
