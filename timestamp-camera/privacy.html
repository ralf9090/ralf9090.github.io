Stampwork – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Stampwork app ("the app"), published by Batuhan Senavci, collects and how it is used.

Your photos and location stay on your phone

  • The app uses the camera to take photos and your location (only while the app is open) to put the address and GPS coordinates on them.
  • Addresses are looked up from your GPS position using Apple's location services. The app itself does not send your photos or location to us or to any server we control.
  • Stamped photos are stored on your device, and in your photo library if you turn on "Also save to Photos". If you choose a photo from your library to stamp, it is read on the device only.
  • Photos and PDF reports leave your phone only when you share them yourself.
  • Deleting the app deletes the photos kept inside it (copies in your photo library stay there).

Information collected by third-party services

  • Advertising (Google AdMob) – a banner in the gallery and settings for users without Premium; never on the camera screen. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, and technical crash/performance diagnostics. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; on iOS you can allow or deny tracking. Google Privacy Policy
  • Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat) – purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app user ID to confirm your subscription. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not see your photos, notes, addresses or location.
  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email in the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

When Google requires a privacy-options entry, open Settings > Advertising privacy choices in the app to review or change consent. Advertising requests pause while choices are changed and cached ads from the previous choices are discarded. Denying tracking does not block the app’s core features or purchases.

  • Turn off camera, location or photo access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • A Premium subscription removes ads.

Retention and service data

Local content remains until you delete it or remove the app, subject to device backups and copies you export. Google and RevenueCat retain service data under their linked privacy policies and applicable transaction, security and legal requirements. Turning off permissions, buying Premium or deleting the app does not automatically delete data previously received by providers. Contact support for access or deletion requests; limited transaction or anonymous app information may be needed to locate a service record. No app account or server copy of your local content is maintained by us.
