What the app does

The app uses the magnetometer built into a compatible phone to display changes in magnetic-field readings. Results depend on the device, its sensor, calibration, surroundings, and distance. The app is an informational tool, not a professional inspection instrument.

A phone magnetometer responds mainly to ferromagnetic materials and magnetic fields. The app does not identify gold, silver, copper, aluminum, coins, or a specific object, and it cannot guarantee that a hidden camera or other device is present or absent. EMF mode measures magnetic field; it does not measure radio-frequency signals such as Wi-Fi or 5G.

Use care

Wall Scanner may help you notice magnetic traces from metal or energized wiring. It does not directly detect wood studs and cannot confirm that a place is safe to drill. Before drilling, check for electrical wires and pipes with suitable professional equipment and follow local safety guidance.

Do not use app readings as a health, radiation-safety, security, or emergency assessment. No measurement or guide result is guaranteed to be accurate or complete.
