How do I record?

Create a script, write or paste your words, and save it. Adjust the scroll speed and text size, choose the camera, and start recording. The text you read on screen is not included in the saved camera video.

The camera or microphone will not open

Check Promptake's camera and microphone permissions in Settings. Close another app that may be using the camera, then try again.

How do I save or share a video?

Open a saved recording to review it. You can trim its beginning and end, use Share to choose a destination, or choose Save to Photos and allow add access. Keep enough free storage for your recordings and exports.

Which features need Plus?

Your first successfully saved recording is free. Plus unlocks additional recordings. Saved videos remain available to review, trim and share when a membership ends. Deleting a recording does not reset the first-recording allowance.

How do I restore a membership?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, open Plus in the app and choose Restore purchases. An expired or refunded subscription does not provide an active membership. Restoring a subscription does not restore video files from another device.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple Account's subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com; refund availability is determined by Apple.

Where are my scripts and recordings?

They are stored locally in the app. Export videos you want to keep elsewhere and manage your device backups. There is no app-operated cloud sync or online recovery account.
