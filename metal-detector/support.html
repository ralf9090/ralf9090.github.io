Metal Detector — Support

Need help? Email batu2525@icloud.com and include your device model and iOS version. Please do not email private measurement photos unless needed for your request.

The reading does not change

Move the phone away from magnets and metal, keep it still during automatic calibration, and then bring the top of the phone slowly toward a nearby ferromagnetic object. Sensor strength and range vary by device. The app cannot identify precious metals or detect every object.

Wall Scanner reports a possible signal

It reports a magnetic-field change that could come from metal or energized wiring. It does not identify the source. Check carefully with suitable professional equipment before drilling.

Camera buttons are unavailable

Camera Mode needs camera permission and a device with a working camera. Open iOS Settings, select Metal Detector, and allow Camera access. Captures are kept in the app; use the save action to add a photo to Photos.

Where are my measurements and captures?

Measurements and Camera Mode captures are stored locally in the app. The app does not sync them to our servers. Items saved to Photos are managed in the Photos app.
