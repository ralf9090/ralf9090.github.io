Notaloom · Privacy

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Microphone and audio analysis

The microphone is used only when you start recording. Your references and takes are analyzed on your device. The app does not upload audio or pitch traces to a server. There is no app account, advertising SDK or analytics SDK.

Local files and backups

References, takes and analysis are stored in the app’s storage on your device. The app reads modification dates of recoverable recordings in this storage to sort them. Operating system and backup settings may apply to these files. An imported file may be copied into the app’s storage.

Sharing and deletion

When you share, the audio file is passed to the system share sheet. The app or file provider you choose may retain a copy under its own terms. Delete takes from the comparison screen. Long-press a phrase in your library to delete it and its takes. Recovered recordings require separate confirmation to delete. Manage exported copies where you saved them.

Purchases

Apple processes payments when purchases are available. Apple-verified transaction information determines membership access. The app does not receive card details. Expiry or refund does not remove access to listen, compare, share or delete existing recordings.

Support

If you voluntarily email us, your address and the information you send are used to answer your request. Audio is not sent automatically. Please send only information needed for support. For support or data requests, contact mobileappssupport0@gmail.com.
