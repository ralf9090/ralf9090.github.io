What the app does

The app plays user-selected tones and sound sweeps through the device speaker. These sounds may help move moisture or dust from a speaker, but results depend on the device and circumstances and are not guaranteed. The app does not measure moisture, inspect or repair hardware, or replace professional service.

Use the app carefully

Follow the instructions shown in the app and stop playback if the sound is uncomfortable or causes concern. Keep the device at a comfortable listening distance. Do not use the app near people or animals who may be sensitive to loud sound. The app is not a substitute for the device manufacturer's care instructions.
