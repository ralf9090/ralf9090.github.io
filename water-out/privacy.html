Water Out — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Water Out - Speaker Cleaner, published by Batuhan Senavci ("the app"), handles information.

Audio and device data

  • The app generates tones on your device and plays them through its speaker. It does not use the microphone, record audio, or send generated sound or microphone data to a server.
  • The app does not create an account, provide cloud sync, or use Firebase or Firebase Analytics.
  • Basic app preferences may be stored locally on your device. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your settings.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain their services. On iOS, you can respond to Apple's tracking permission prompt and manage tracking permissions in Settings. Consent choices may also be presented where required. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Your choices

You can manage tracking permissions in iOS Settings. Ads may still be shown if you decline tracking. Removing the app deletes its local app data, subject to copies included in device backups.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
