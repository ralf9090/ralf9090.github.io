Privacy Policy · Last updated October 17, 2026

Promptake stores your scripts and original recordings on your device. It does not upload them to an app-operated server. No app account is required.

Scripts and recordings

The app saves your scripts, recording files and library details in its local storage. Your device backup settings may include this data in an Apple or computer backup. Promptake does not operate its own cloud library or sync service.

Camera, microphone and Photos

Camera and microphone permission lets you record the videos you choose to start. The app requests permission to add to Photos only when you choose Save to Photos; it does not need to read your photo library. You can manage permissions in your device's Settings.

Sharing and exports

Trimming creates a separate exported video and keeps the original recording. Choosing Share gives the selected video to the app or destination you select. Copies saved in Photos, Files, backups or other services are managed by those destinations and their policies.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. Promptake uses RevenueCat to validate and manage Plus membership access and measure subscription performance. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription history, and technical device and app information. Apple handles payment; Promptake and RevenueCat do not receive payment card details. Scripts, audio and videos are never sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for information about the service. Apple's services handle payment under Apple's privacy policy.

Crash diagnostics

Promptake uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes. Technical reports may include stack traces, device model, operating system and app versions, installation or session identifiers, and diagnostic information about the app. Promptake does not attach script text, audio, video, payment details, names or email addresses to these reports. Reports are not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Crash reporting is enabled by default. You can turn it off using Share crash reports in the app settings. Turning it off stops future report collection; it does not remove reports already received by the service. See Firebase privacy information for how the service handles diagnostic data. System-level diagnostics shared with Apple are controlled by your device settings.

Advertising and your choices

The free version uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial and app-open ads. You may also choose to watch a rewarded ad for one additional successfully saved recording. Closing a rewarded ad before earning its reward does not grant a recording credit. Plus members do not see advertising.

Google's advertising SDK may process device identifiers (including the advertising identifier when permitted), approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views and interactions, app activity, crash information and performance diagnostics. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and related analytics. Some identifiers and advertising activity may be linked to a device. Promptake does not provide script text, audio, video, names, email addresses or purchase details for advertising, and does not request location permission.

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before advertising requests. You can reopen Advertising privacy choices in Promptake Settings when that option is required for your region. Tracking across other companies' apps and websites requires permission through Apple's tracking prompt; manage it in device Settings. Refusing tracking does not prevent use of the app or purchase of Plus. Ads may still appear subject to your privacy choices and applicable requirements.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. No Google Analytics SDK is included in Promptake. Turning off Share crash reports controls Firebase Crashlytics and does not control the advertising SDK's diagnostics.

Deleting your content

You can delete scripts and recordings in the app. Deleting a script keeps its recordings; delete those recordings separately if you want to remove them too. Deleting content from the app does not delete previously exported or shared copies. Manage backups and exported copies in their respective services. Deleting content does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Service providers must protect data to the same or a higher standard described in this policy and required by Apple's App Review Guidelines. Purchase records are retained as needed to validate subscriptions, restore access, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Diagnostic and advertising records are retained under the linked providers' retention policies. For a request to access or delete service data associated with your use of Promptake, contact mobileappssupport0@gmail.com. We may need limited information to locate an anonymous record. Some records may need to be kept for legal obligations or transaction integrity; we will explain any limitation when responding. Turning off future collection or deleting the app does not automatically delete records already held by providers.

When you contact support

If you email support, the support team receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. These are used to respond to your request and are handled by the email service. Do not send private scripts or recordings unless you choose to include material needed to explain a problem. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, contact mobileappssupport0@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes.
