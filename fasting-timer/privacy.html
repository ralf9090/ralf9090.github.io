Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Fasting Timer helps you track fasting windows. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information stored on your device

Your fasting records, plans, notes, water entries, and weight entries are stored locally on your device. The app does not send these records to our servers.

Analytics

With Firebase Analytics, the app may send app usage events such as onboarding completion, plan selection, starting or ending a fast, opening the paywall, purchase completion, and tapping a locked feature. These events help us understand app usage. They do not include your fasting history, weight, water entries, or journal notes.

Advertising

Free features may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process device and advertising information under Google's privacy terms and your consent choices. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. RevenueCat helps the app validate purchase status and manage subscription access. We do not receive your payment card details.

Children and contact

Fasting Timer is not intended for children under 13. For privacy questions, contact batu2525@icloud.com.

Apple privacy information: apple.com/legal/privacy. Google privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.
