Sleep Recorder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sleep Recorder app ("the app"), published by Batuhan Senavci, collects and how it is used.

Your recordings stay on your phone

  • The app uses the microphone only after you tap Start Recording. It saves short audio clips when it hears a sound.
  • Clips, sound labels (snoring, talking, other) and sleep-talk transcripts are created and stored on your device only. Sound analysis and speech-to-text run on the device using Apple's on-device frameworks; your audio is never uploaded to us or to any server we control.
  • Clips leave your phone only if you choose to share one yourself.
  • Deleting the app deletes your recordings.

Information collected by third-party services

  • Advertising (Google AdMob) – shown to users without a Premium subscription. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; on iOS you can allow or deny tracking. Google Privacy Policy
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics) – anonymous usage events (for example, that a recording was started or a screen was opened) and basic device information, used to improve the app. No audio or transcripts are sent.
  • Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat) – purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app user ID to confirm your subscription. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not access your recordings or transcripts.
  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email in the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off microphone or speech recognition access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • A Premium subscription removes ads.

Not a medical device

Sleep Recorder describes sounds your phone heard. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
