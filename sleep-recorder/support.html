Common questions

The app didn't record anything. Check that microphone access is on (iOS Settings > Sleep Recorder), keep the app recording until morning, and try a higher sensitivity.

Where are my recordings? On your phone only. Open the Nights tab. Recordings from older versions appear marked "imported".

How do I restore Premium? In the app open Settings > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Sleep Recorder.
