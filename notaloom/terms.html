Content and measurement scope

Use only your own recordings or references you have permission to use. The three starter melodies are original synthesized exercises. The app shows measurable pitch and relative timing differences in short, single-voice phrases. It does not provide live pitch tracking, a song catalog or polyphonic music separation. Read the clarity and coverage messages with each result. Results are not professional singing instruction or a medical assessment.

Membership

Complete practice on original exercises is free. Adding your references and recording new takes on them requires membership. When live plans are available, Apple supplies your local price and billing period on the purchase screen. Subscriptions renew automatically for their billing period unless canceled. Manage cancellation through Apple’s subscription settings. Existing recordings remain available to listen, compare, share and delete after membership ends.
