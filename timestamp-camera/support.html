Common questions

The stamp shows coordinates but no address. The address is looked up for exactly where you are. Without internet, or right after you moved, the stamp shows coordinates only until the new address arrives — it never shows the address of a previous place. Tap the location line under the viewfinder to look it up again.

The location is off or not precise. Allow location "While Using the App" with Precise Location on (iOS Settings > Timestamp Camera > Location).

Photos are not in my photo library. Turn on Settings > Also save to Photos in the app and allow photo access when asked. Photos are always kept inside the app as well.

Can I stamp a photo I already took? Yes, with Premium: Gallery > the add-photo button. The stamp uses the date and place saved in that photo; the original is not changed.

How do I restore Premium? In the app open Settings > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Timestamp Camera.
