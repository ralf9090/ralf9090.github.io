Headache Diary Terms

Apple’s standard end user license agreement applies. Headache Diary is for personal record keeping and does not diagnose or recommend treatment.

If offered, subscriptions are charged to your Apple Account and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscription settings. The purchase screen shows the applicable period and localized price before purchase.

Core diary logging, history, CSV export and backups are available without a subscription. Premium provides detailed PDF reports and removes advertising.
